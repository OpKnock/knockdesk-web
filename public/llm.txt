# KnockDesk

> KnockDesk is a free, open-source desktop AI assistant that runs entirely on your PC. Your keys, your data, your control.

- Local-first: no cloud backend, no account, no tracking
- Voice chat with 9 distinct AI personas
- Screen capture and computer automation
- Local memory system that learns your preferences
- Works with your existing Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or OpenAI-compatible API keys
- Windows 10+ (macOS in development)
